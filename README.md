## Featured Work

### Navly

A Canada immigration planning platform that helps users understand immigration pathways, track important dates, and receive clear next-step guidance.

**Focus:** personalized onboarding, user dashboards, reminders, structured guidance, and AI-assisted workflows.

### Meet Dew

An embeddable AI front-desk assistant designed for small businesses and portfolio websites.

**Focus:** conversational user experience, lead support, business workflows, and modern AI integrations.

### Elika Beauty

A custom booking platform built to help a real salon reduce reliance on third-party marketplaces and improve direct bookings.

**Results highlighted in the case study:**

* 40% increase in 60 days
* Fully automated booking workflow
* Built and shipped independently in one month
* Higher conversion than Instagram direct messages

### LendItOut

A peer-to-peer lending platform for everyday items.

**Focus:** listings, user workflows, authentication, and database-driven features.

### Community of Guardians

A community platform for neighbourhood safety and coordination.

**Focus:** full-stack collaboration, community workflows, and practical product design.

### Forge

A mobile simulation app that introduces high school students to skilled trades through interactive career exploration.

**Focus:** mobile UX, TypeScript, full-stack architecture, and AI-assisted learning experiences.
